# Content playbook — what to make, and what to stop making

Based on the last 95 Instagram posts (24 Jun – 14 Oct 2026), reach measured on the most recent 60.

---

## The one number that should change what you shoot

| Format | Posts | Avg reach | Avg engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Video / reel** | 45 | **356** | **18** |
| Carousel | 3 | 117 | 7 |
| Still image | 12 | 116 | 4 |

**Video reaches three times as far as a still and gets four times the engagement.** A fifth of
posts are stills, and they are doing roughly a third of the work. That is the single biggest
change available: the same effort shot as 15 seconds of video instead of a designed graphic is
worth three times the audience.

Two themes carry everything: **real events with real children in them**, and **AI/technology as
something students actually touch**. The top post is children dancing — not a designed asset.

### What did not work

| Reach | Post |
|---|---|
| 107, 97 | Untitled stills |
| 91 | Independence Day greeting |
| 90 | Rakshabandhan greeting |
| 79 | *"AI can give you answers. But can it build the skills…"* |
| 75 | Carousel on personalised learning |

**Festival greetings and quote-cards are the weakest thing on the account.** Every competitor
posts the same Rakshabandhan graphic on the same day and the feed ignores all of them. They cost
design time and return about a quarter of the reach of a phone video of a classroom.

**Stop making:** festival greeting graphics, standalone motivational quote cards, text-only
"why choose us" stills, carousels.

## The weekly slate

Five posts a week, all video unless stated. The team shoots on phones; nothing here needs a
designer.

| Slot | What | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon — Result** | One student, one number, 15s. *"Pankaj, Class 9, 97% in his half-yearly."* Student says one line about what changed. | School test submissions in the rewards app — 69 verified, barely used |
| **Tue — Inside the classroom** | 20–30s of something actually happening: an experiment, a doubt being solved, the smartboard in use | Shoot live, no setup |
| **Wed — Tech** | DIWA robot, VR headsets, plasma ball, AI tools. Students using them, not a product shot | Already your second-best theme |
| **Thu — Student voice** | One student or parent, 20s, one question: *"What changed since you joined?"* | Reception, after class |
| **Fri — Event / behind the scenes** | Whatever happened that week. Dandiya proved this is the strongest format you have | Phone footage, cut fast |

**Cadence check:** you are posting about six times a week. Five good videos beat six posts where
two are greeting cards.

- **360 Dandiya photographs** — only 12 have been used anywhere. The dance-floor frames are the

- **Bihar AI Summit** — you *organised* a state AI summit. That is a stronger credential than any

- **Qlass Coins** — 160 students holding coins, rewards actually being claimed. A student

## Measurement — and an honest limit

Reach and engagement are available and are what this document is built on. **Admissions data is
not.** The ad account refuses the API token (`Ad account owner has NOT granted ads_management`),
so there is no way from here to see which content produces enquiries.

That matters: reach is not the goal, admissions are. Everything above says what gets *seen*. If
someone fixes the ad account permission, the next version of this can say what gets *enrolled* —
and that might well reorder the whole list.

Until then, the cheap substitute: ask every enquiring parent where they heard of you, and write
it down. Twenty answers is enough to beat any amount of reach data.

---

## Review this monthly

Re-run the numbers at the start of each month and move slots that stop earning their place. The
data took one API call; the judgement is the part that needs a person.
